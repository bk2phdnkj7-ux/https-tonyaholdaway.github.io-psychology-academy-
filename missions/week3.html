Week 3 Biopsychology Mission

The Brain Institute

Restore the Neural Network

An unexpected systems failure has interrupted communication throughout the Brain Institute. Seven biological systems are offline. Your mission is to investigate each laboratory, restore the neural pathways, and bring the Institute back online.

Brain Institute System Alert

Neural communication is unstable.

The Institute cannot fully restore operations until all seven research systems are functioning.

Each successful mission restores one pathway and reveals one character of the Institute recovery code.

Mission Briefing

Complete all seven Brain Institute missions. You may complete them in any order.

Each mission includes an optional hint and immediate feedback. There is no timer and you may attempt questions more than once.

Your progress and Brain Lab Notebook entries are saved automatically in the current browser.

Learning Objectives

By completing the Brain Institute mission, you will practice how to:

  • Identify major structures of a neuron and their functions.
  • Explain how neurons communicate using electrical and chemical signals.
  • Recognize the role of neurotransmitters in neural communication.
  • Distinguish major divisions of the nervous system.
  • Connect major brain structures with broad psychological functions.
  • Describe how the endocrine system communicates through hormones.
  • Recognize how biology, genetics, environment, and experience interact to influence behavior.

Neuroscience Resource Terminal
