Mission Briefing

  1. Choose a room and try at least one control at its interactive desk.
  2. Solve both locks to collect that room’s letter. Hints and retries are welcome.
  3. Collect all eight fragments, solve the final case, and use the letters to open the Chronicle Door.
  4. Enter your name, download your Week 9 completion badge, and upload it to your Canvas assignment.

Your textbook is part of the adventure. Every challenge includes an OpenStax Research File link to the section it comes from. Open a reading whenever you need help, then return to apply the idea.

No timer, speed score, or penalty for retries. Allow about 35–45 minutes. All controls work with a keyboard; dragging is never required. Animations illustrate concepts, not actual research data, diagnoses, or predictions of anyone’s development.

Use fictional examples throughout; no personal or family history is required. The final room lets you choose a general life-transition activity or a brief, non-graphic Section 9.4 activity. Either route earns the same fragment.

Progress is saved in this browser when storage is available. Download your notebook before changing devices. Your badge must be submitted in Canvas; this page does not send grades automatically.

Your Learning Goals

  • Connect physical, cognitive, and psychosocial development with context.
  • Recognize prenatal phases and explain examples of learning in infancy and childhood.
  • Apply Piaget’s, Erikson’s, and Kohlberg’s frameworks while recognizing their limits.
  • Explain development across adolescence and adulthood without treating age as destiny.
  • Consider individual preferences and cultural context during life transitions.
