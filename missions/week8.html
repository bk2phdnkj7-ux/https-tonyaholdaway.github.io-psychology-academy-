The Lost Archive

The Academy’s memory archive has lost its catalog. Seven rooms hold the fragments that can restore it. Become a Memory Archivist: investigate how we learn, store, retrieve, and sometimes reconstruct the past.

Mission Briefing

Open any room, explore its interactive desk, and solve both locks to earn a clue letter. Collect all seven fragments, solve the final case, and arrange the letters to name the process of retaining information over time.

No timer and no penalty for retries. All controls work with a keyboard; no dragging or quick reactions are required. Read the feedback, use hints, and try again. Allow about 35–50 minutes, or return later. These demonstrations illustrate memory concepts; they do not measure or diagnose your memory.

Progress and notes are saved in this browser when storage is available. Download your notebook before changing devices or using a shared computer. This activity does not automatically submit a Canvas grade.

Interactive desks invite you to explore before answering. You earn each fragment by solving the two locks; your recall results do not affect completion. The notebook keeps your most recent practice summaries, reflections, and study plan.

Your Learning Goals

  • Distinguish encoding, storage, retrieval, and major memory systems.
  • Connect memory processes with interacting brain structures.
  • Recognize amnesia, interference, and the influence of later information.
  • Use chunking, meaningful connections, retrieval practice, and spacing in a study plan.

Use your textbook as an investigation tool. Every room includes an “Open the OpenStax Research File” link to the Chapter 8 section behind its challenge. Open it whenever you need help, then return here to apply what you read. Using the readings and hints is part of the mission.
