Where evidence builds better workplaces.

Your Mission

Open a workshop, inspect its instruments, and solve both locks. Each restored workshop releases a letter. Use the eight letters in workshop order to complete the final workplace challenge.

Work at your own pace. A missed choice gives a hint. Use the navigation under each workshop to continue or revisit unfinished work.

What You Will Practice

  • Distinguish three I/O lenses.
  • Analyze job tasks and KSAs.
  • Compare selection evidence.
  • Evaluate training and feedback.
  • Examine satisfaction facets.
  • Compare leadership approaches.
  • Interpret workplace culture.
  • Apply human factors design.
