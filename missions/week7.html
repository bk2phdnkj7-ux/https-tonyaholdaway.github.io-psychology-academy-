Week 7 Psychology Mission

The Thinking Workshop

The Missing Mindprint

The Academy’s Mindprint, a guide to thoughtful problem solving, has vanished. Seven workshop rooms hold its fragments. Rebuild the missing pattern by investigating concepts, language, reasoning, creativity, intelligence, and evidence.

Academy Atmosphere

Optional instrumental music and moving thought fragments set the scene. Sound and animation are never required; use the controls below to adjust them. Reduced-motion device settings are also respected.

Music is off.

Mission Briefing

Open any room, explore its interactive desk, and solve both locks to earn a clue letter. Collect all seven fragments, solve the final case, and arrange the letters to name a sudden realization of a solution.

No timer and no penalty for retries. All controls work with a keyboard; no dragging or quick reactions are required. Read the feedback, use hints, and try again. Allow about 30–45 minutes. Explorations are teaching illustrations, not intelligence tests or real research results.

Progress and notes are saved in this browser when storage is available. Download your notebook before changing devices or using a shared computer. This activity does not automatically submit a Canvas grade.

Interactive desks invite you to explore before answering. You earn each fragment by solving the two locks; creative writing is saved for reflection and is not automatically graded.

Your Learning Goals

  • Distinguish concepts, prototypes, and schemas.
  • Explain how language structure affects meaning.
  • Apply problem-solving strategies and recognize cognitive biases.
  • Compare divergent and convergent thinking and intelligence theories.
  • Interpret intelligence measures in context.

Use your textbook as an investigation tool. Each room includes an “Open the OpenStax Research File” link to the section behind its challenge. Open it whenever you need help, then return here to apply what you read. Using the readings and hints is part of the mission.

The Mindprint Door

Collect all seven evidence fragments to activate the Mindprint Door.

A student proposes several ways to make a study space quieter, selects the option that fits their budget, and revises “Stop being annoying!” to “Could we have a quiet hour from 7 to 8?” A teammate then claims that one online quiz reveals each person's permanent intellectual potential.

Hint: A sudden realization of how to solve a problem. Seven letters, begins with I. Both I fragments belong in your answer.

Your seven evidence fragments will help you restore the Mindprint.

Mindprint Restored: Investigator Badge

You recovered all seven fragments and restored the Mindprint. Download your notebook and optional badge. Follow your Canvas assignment directions for any required submission.
