Learning Objectives

  • Describe consciousness and changes in awareness.
  • Explain the role of circadian rhythms in sleep and wakefulness.
  • Recognize major NREM and REM sleep patterns.
  • Connect sleep with learning and memory.
  • Differentiate several common sleep problems and disorders.
  • Evaluate scientific explanations of dreams and altered states.
  • Apply sleep and consciousness concepts to realistic scenarios.
