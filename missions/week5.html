The Perception Gallery

Nothing Is Quite What It Seems

Something unusual is happening inside the Academy gallery. Visitors are seeing patterns that are not physically present, missing objects directly in front of them, interpreting the same sensory information differently, and insisting that humans have exactly five senses. Your assignment is to investigate what the sensory systems detect and what the brain ultimately perceives.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish sensation from perception.
  • Explain absolute threshold and difference threshold.
  • Describe sensory adaptation and selective attention.
  • Explain how sensory receptors and the nervous system contribute to perception.
  • Recognize that human sensory experience extends beyond the traditional five-sense model.
  • Describe how multiple sensory systems work together.
  • Apply Gestalt principles to perceptual organization.
  • Evaluate how expectations, context, attention, and prior experience influence perception.
