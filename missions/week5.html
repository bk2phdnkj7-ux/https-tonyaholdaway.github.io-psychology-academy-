Your Mission

Enter each gallery, use its interactive instrument, and solve two knowledge locks. Every completed gallery releases one letter fragment. Collect all seven fragments to reveal the word that opens the final chamber.

There is no timer. A wrong answer simply gives you a useful hint, and you may retry as often as you need. Your progress and notebook entries save in this browser.

Look for the “OpenStax connection” in every gallery. It identifies the section of Psychology 2e, Chapter 5 that supports the activity.

What You Will Practice

  • Distinguish sensation from perception.
  • Explain transduction and sensory thresholds.
  • Connect wave properties with sight and sound.
  • Trace light and sound through sensory systems.
  • Compare rods and cones.
  • Identify senses beyond the traditional five.
  • Apply attention and Gestalt principles.
  • Explain how context shapes perception.
