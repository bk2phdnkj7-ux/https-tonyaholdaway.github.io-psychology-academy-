Your Mission

Enter each laboratory, operate its interactive instrument, and solve two knowledge locks. Every action and correct answer sends energy to the progress display. Complete a room to reveal one letter.

Collect all seven letters, solve the final synthesis case, and enter the word that completes: “A response acquired through experience has been _______.”

There is no timer and no penalty for retries. Navigation buttons beneath each room move you forward without requiring you to scroll back to the map.

Each laboratory identifies its OpenStax connection. The visual demonstrations are simplified teaching models aligned with Chapter 6; they are not real experimental data.

What You Will Practice

  • Distinguish classical, operant, and observational learning.
  • Identify conditioned and unconditioned stimuli and responses.
  • Explain extinction and spontaneous recovery.
  • Compare generalization and discrimination.
  • Classify reinforcement and punishment.
  • Distinguish reinforcement schedules.
  • Apply shaping and modeling to new skills.

A Safe Behavioral Science Laboratory

This mission uses neutral examples involving studying, skills, reminders, tokens, and practice. The interactive instruments illustrate psychological principles without asking you to disclose distressing experiences or imitate historical animal research.
