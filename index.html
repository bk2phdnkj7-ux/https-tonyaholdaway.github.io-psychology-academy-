A Message from Dr. Holdaway

Psychology becomes more meaningful when we move beyond memorizing definitions and begin asking questions about why people think, feel, learn, remember, develop, and behave the way they do.

Use Psychology Academy as a place to explore those questions. Try things. Make predictions. Use evidence. Make mistakes. Revisit ideas. Stay curious.

Every destination you enter is another opportunity to understand psychology and perhaps understand a little more about the world around you.
