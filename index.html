Welcome, Psychology Explorer!

I am Dr. Holdaway, and I am excited you are here. Psychology is not simply something we read about. It is something we experience every day.

Throughout the semester, you will enter different areas of the Academy to investigate behavior, solve mysteries, test ideas, examine evidence, and connect psychology to the world around you.

Twelve Academy destinations are now open: Weeks 1–12! Our newest adventure is the Social Psychology Village, where seven locations reveal how social situations, relationships, and group influences shape human behavior. Weeks 13–16 will open as our semester journey continues.

A Message from Dr. Holdaway

Psychology becomes more meaningful when we move beyond memorizing definitions and begin asking questions about why people think, feel, learn, remember, develop, and behave the way they do.

Use Psychology Academy as a place to explore those questions. Try things. Make predictions. Use evidence. Make mistakes. Revisit ideas. Stay curious.

Every destination you enter is another opportunity to understand psychology and perhaps understand a little more about the world around you.
